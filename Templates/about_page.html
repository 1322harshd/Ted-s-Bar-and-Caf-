Welcome to Ted’s Bar and Café

Where tradition brews and community blends.

    Nestled in the heart of the city,Ted’s Bar 
    and Café is your cozy neighbourhood spot 
    where warmth meets flavour. We serve locally 
    roasted coffee, hand-crafted drinks, and 
    freshly made comfort food in a welcoming
    space that feels just like home. Whether 
    you’re grabbing a quick espresso or spending 
    a slow afternoon with friends, Ted’s is the 
    place to be.
    
    Ted’s Bar and Café began in 2015 with a simple
    dream: to create a friendly place where coffee
    and community come together. What started as 
    a humble coffee counter has grown into a full-
    service café and bar, known for its signature 
    blends, brunches, and buzzing atmosphere. Named
    after founder Ted Thompson, the café still 
    honors his philosophy—“good coffee, good vibes,
    and great people.”
